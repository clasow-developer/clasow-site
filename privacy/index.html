Privacy Policy

Clasow (com.clasow) · Effective 18 September 2026 · Last updated 24 September 2026

Clasow is a student academic app that helps you keep your class timetable and track your own attendance. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

How we use it

Only to operate the app for you: to sign you in, show your timetable, record the attendance you mark, and calculate your attendance percentage. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not build an advertising or marketing profile of you.

Reminders

Clasow reminds you to record your classes. To make those reminders useful rather than annoying, the app keeps a note of what its own reminders did.

That note contains: that a reminder was sent and which of its messages was used; whether you opened it, dismissed it, or did nothing; and whether you recorded any classes afterwards.

The detailed note stays on your phone. It is never uploaded. Only a daily summary — how many classes there were, how many you recorded, how many reminders were sent and how many worked — is stored in your Clasow account.

This is used for two things and nothing else: choosing when to remind you, and letting us see whether reminders are helping students keep their attendance up to date. It is never shared with anyone, never sold, and never used for advertising.

You can turn this off. It is on when you install the app; switching it off in Settings stops the note being kept and stops the summary being sent. Reminders still work.

Other students

Your attendance records are yours. Clasow does not show your attendance to other students, to teachers, or to your college. Access is restricted at the database level so that one account cannot read another account's records.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data and academic records are deleted from our live database — and that includes the reminder summaries described above. See Delete your account.

The detailed reminder note on your phone is not kept indefinitely: it holds roughly the last six weeks and older entries are discarded as new ones arrive. Signing out of the app deletes it outright.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and all your data — in the app, or by email. Full instructions: Delete your account.
  • Get a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it — email us.
  • Turn off reminder records — in Settings. Reminders keep working; the app simply stops keeping a note of how you respond to them.
  • Uninstall — this removes the app but not your stored account. Use the deletion path if you want the data gone.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Access to records is enforced per-account at the database level. We do not store passwords — sign-in is handled by Google.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If you believe your account has been accessed by someone else, email us.

Children

Clasow is intended for college and university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Testing releases

Clasow is currently distributed as a closed test on Google Play. Test builds may change often and may contain defects. This policy applies to test builds exactly as it does to public releases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects what we collect, tell you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
