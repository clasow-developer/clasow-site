Privacy Policy

Clasow (com.clasow) · Effective 18 September 2026 · Last updated 6 October 2026

Clasow is a student academic app that helps you keep your class timetable and track your own attendance. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

How we use it

Only to operate the app for you: to sign you in, show your timetable, record the attendance you mark, and calculate your attendance percentage. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not build an advertising or marketing profile of you.

Reminders

Clasow reminds you to record your classes. To make those reminders useful rather than annoying, the app keeps a note of what its own reminders did.

That note contains: that a reminder was sent and which of its messages was used; whether your screen was on, and whether Do Not Disturb was on, when it arrived; whether you opened it, dismissed it, chose Later, or did nothing, and how long you took; whether you recorded any classes afterwards; how many times the end-of-day reminder had come back by then; each time you open the app, and whether you came from a reminder or from the app's icon; and when you switch a type of Clasow reminder off or on in your phone's settings.

The detailed note stays on your phone. It is never uploaded. Only a daily summary is stored in your Clasow account:

  • how many classes you had, how many you recorded, and the hour in which you recorded the last one;
  • how many reminders were planned and sent, how many you opened or dismissed, and how many were followed by a recorded class;
  • the typical (median) time you took to respond to a reminder — a length of time, never a time of day;
  • how many reminders arrived while your screen was off, or while Do Not Disturb was on;
  • how many times you opened the app from a reminder, and how many times from its icon;
  • how often the end-of-day reminder came back, and on which return you opened it or finished the day;
  • which version of the app recorded the day;
  • running totals of the same counts for each kind of reminder and each of its messages.

This is used for two things and nothing else: choosing when to remind you, and letting us see whether reminders are helping students keep their attendance up to date. It is never shared with anyone, never sold, and never used for advertising.

You can turn this off. It is on when you install the app; switching it off in Settings stops the note being kept and stops the summary being sent. Reminders still work.

App version and notifications

From version 0.1.9 (October 2026), the app also tells your Clasow account three facts about how it is set up on your phone: which version of Clasow is installed; whether Clasow is allowed to show notifications; and which types of Clasow reminder are switched off in your phone's settings. Only the latest answer is kept, with the time it last changed.

We use it to know which version each student is running, so that a fix reaches everyone, and whether reminders can reach you at all. It describes the app's set-up, not what you do in it. It is never shared, never sold, and never used for advertising.

It is sent only when one of the three facts changes, and only while you are signed in. The reminder switch in Settings does not turn it off, because it describes the app's set-up on your phone rather than how you respond to reminders. It is deleted with your account.

The early access form on this website

Separately from the app, clasow.com links to a sign-up form for people who want to hear when Clasow is ready to install. It is a Google Form. Filling it in is optional, and you do not need the app to do so.

The form asks for: whether you are a student, faculty or other; your name; your Gmail address; your college; your roll or registration number; your branch; which parts of Clasow interest you; a rating; and anything you choose to write to us.

We use these details only to contact you about Clasow and to understand what people want from it. They are not sold, not shared with your college, and not used for advertising. The answers are stored by Google, in a spreadsheet only the developer can open.

To have your answers deleted, email clasow.developer@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.

Beyond these two service providers, your data is not disclosed to anyone, except where we are required to by law.

Other students

Your attendance records are yours. Clasow does not show your attendance to other students, to teachers, or to your college. Access is restricted at the database level so that one account cannot read another account's records.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data and academic records are deleted from our live database — and that includes the reminder summaries and the app version and notification settings described above. See Delete your account.

The detailed reminder note on your phone is not kept indefinitely: it holds roughly the last six weeks and older entries are discarded as new ones arrive. Signing out of the app deletes it outright.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and all your data — in the app, or by email. Full instructions: Delete your account.
  • Get a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it — email us.
  • Turn off reminder records — in Settings. Reminders keep working; the app simply stops keeping a note of how you respond to them. This switch does not cover your app version and notification settings, which are kept until you delete your account.
  • Uninstall — this removes the app but not your stored account. Use the deletion path if you want the data gone.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Access to records is enforced per-account at the database level. We do not store passwords — sign-in is handled by Google.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If you believe your account has been accessed by someone else, email us.

Children

Clasow is intended for college and university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Testing releases

Clasow is currently distributed as a closed test on Google Play. Test builds may change often and may contain defects. This policy applies to test builds exactly as it does to public releases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects what we collect, tell you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
