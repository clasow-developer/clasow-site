Delete your Clasow account

Clasow (com.clasow) · Last updated 18 September 2026

You can delete your Clasow account and your data at any time. There are two ways, and you do not need the app installed to use the second one.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open Clasow
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Open Settings
  4. Tap Delete account
  5. Confirm

Your account and records are deleted and you are signed out immediately.

Option 2 — By email, without the app

Email clasow.developer@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Clasow account”.

We use the sending address to verify the request is yours, so please send it from the account you signed in with. We will confirm once the deletion is done, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

Backups are rotated on a schedule. A copy of deleted data may persist in an encrypted backup for a short period before it is overwritten. It is not restored to the live system.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your attendance history cannot be recovered afterwards. If you only want to stop using Clasow for a while, uninstalling the app is enough — your data stays and you can sign back in later.

Something not working?

If the in-app option fails, or you no longer have access to your original email address, write to clasow.developer@gmail.com and we will help you verify your identity another way.
