In the app today

  • Your real timetableYour branch, section and week, as it is actually taught. Edit it when your classes change.
  • Attendance you mark yourselfMark each class present or absent. See where each subject stands and how many classes you can still miss.
  • Reminders that fit your dayA nudge to record your classes, at a moment that suits your schedule.
  • College forms, filled for youLeave, bonafide, bank challan and semester registration forms for PMEC, filled from details you enter once.

What’s next

Working prototypes, shown at Robo Expo 2026. Not in the app yet.

  • Forms signed on the phoneSend a form to your teacher and get it back signed, with a tracking code on the paper.
