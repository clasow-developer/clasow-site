Clasow

Your timetable, and the attendance you actually have.

Clasow is a student academic app. It holds your real class timetable, lets you mark each class present or absent, and tells you where your attendance percentage stands — including how many classes you can still miss, or how many you need to recover.

Where it is

Clasow is in closed testing on Google Play and is not yet publicly available. A public release is planned. If you have a test invitation link, it will take you to the Play Store.

Who makes it

Clasow is built by Piyus Patra, an independent developer and engineering student in India. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of any college or university.

Why it exists

Attendance rules decide whether students can sit exams, and most students track them on paper, in a notes app, or not at all — and find out too late. Clasow exists to make that number honest, current, and visible before it becomes a problem.
